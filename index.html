AI Research Engineer

Lumi-VN · Hanoi, Vietnam

Voice agents built on Speech-LLMs

  • Full-duplex dialogue: developing a spoken dialogue model that listens and speaks at the same time, handling natural turn-taking, interruptions and backchannels.
  • Speech-LLM architecture: connected audio encoders (Qwen3-Omni, NVIDIA Nemotron) to frozen LLM decoders through projection layers (linear / MLP) and learned soft-prompt tokens, trained in stages: alignment warm-up → ASR → spoken question answering.
  • Target-speaker ASR: conditioning the model on a target speaker to transcribe only their speech in overlapping, noisy and far-field multi-speaker audio, with dedicated data augmentation.
  • Speaker diarization: joint transcription and diarization (“who spoke when”) with timestamps for long-form multi-speaker conversations, fine-tuning end-to-end transcribe-diarize models.
  • Real-time streaming: built Voice Activity Detection (VAD) and end-of-turn detection for low-latency voice interaction.
  • Efficient training & evaluation: 4-bit (NF4) quantized training of 30B-scale decoders on a single GPU, model serving, and benchmarking on VoiceBench.
